Yefan Zhi / 支业繁 Ph.D. Candidate Department of Architecture University of Pennsylvania             

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Architecture at the University of Pennsylvania where I am part of the Polyhedral Structures Laboratory (PSL). My research interests are in toolpath design for 3D concrete printing, its applications in structural systems, form-finding by graphic statics, and methods of multi-material extrusion. I am especially excited about exploiting computational geometry and graph algorithms to optimize the energy and material efficiency of additive manufacturing processes across the architecture, engineering, and construction industry.

I am the author of the 3D printing slicing software Ovenbird and a recipient of the IASS Hangai Prize. My Ph.D. advisor is Dr. Masoud Akbarzadeh. Previously I studied at Tsinghua University and received a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture, supervised by Dr. Weixin Huang, and a Bachelor’s degree in Sociology, supervised by Dr. Yue Du.

At the University of Pennsylvania, my research is supported by the U.S. Department of Energy, the U.S. National Science Foundation, and the Ph.D. Grant of the Weitzman School. Besides research, I write blog posts, bird watch, and run marathons. I designed the new PSL website in January 2023 and have continued to update it.
